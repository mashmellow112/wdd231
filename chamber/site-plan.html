Site Plan: Jinja Chamber of Commerce

A hub for local business, community, and discovery in Jinja.

Site Name

Proposed Name: Jinja Chamber of Commerce

Reason for Name: The name is direct, professional, and clearly communicates the site's purpose as the official online presence for the Jinja business community.

Optional Domain Availability: jinjachamber.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of the Jinja Chamber of Commerce website is to foster local economic growth by connecting businesses with the community. It serves as a comprehensive directory of member businesses, a source for local events, and a platform for showcasing premier "spotlight" members. The site aims to be an essential resource for both residents and visitors looking to engage with and support Jinja's vibrant business landscape.

Target Audience Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: "I'm a tourist visiting Jinja and looking for adventure tour companies. How can I find a list of reputable, local options?"
  • Scenario 2: "As a local business owner, I want to see which companies are Gold members to explore potential B2B partnerships."

Color Scheme

The color palette is derived from the project's CSS (`base.css`) to ensure design consistency. It uses professional and inviting tones.

Primary
#004d5a
Secondary
#b45309
Accent
#f4f6f8
  • Primary Color (#004d5a): Used for main headings, navigation elements, and key interface components.
  • Secondary Color (#b45309): Used for calls-to-action, buttons, and important accents like featured borders.
  • Accent Color (#f4f6f8): Used for the main page background to provide a clean, bright canvas for content.

Typography

The font choices are based on the project's CSS (`base.css`) for readability and a modern aesthetic.

Heading Font

Montserrat (700 Bold)

Used for all page headers (<h1>, <h2>, <h3>) to establish a strong visual hierarchy.

Body Font

Open Sans (400 Regular)

Used for all body copy, lists, and detailed text to ensure high legibility across all devices.
